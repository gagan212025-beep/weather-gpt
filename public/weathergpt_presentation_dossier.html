Verbal Hook for Judges: "Every morning, billions of people look at an app that says '29°C and 60% chance of rain'. 2 The Problem: Why Traditional Weather Apps Fail
  • Information Overload Without Clarity: Displays barometric curves, dew points, and radar maps that 95% of users cannot interpret within 5 seconds.
  • The "Single Percentage" Fallacy: A generic "70% rain" number conceals whether it is a light 15-minute shower or a localized 40mm deluge during evening rush hour.
  • Context Blindness: A student with books in a backpack, a farmer planning overhead pesticide spraying, and a highway commuter receive identical, unadapted numbers.
  • Black-Box Predictions: Users cannot see whether major global forecast models (ECMWF, GFS, IMD) agree or strongly diverge on rainfall timing.

4 The Technical Architecture: Anti-Hallucination Pipeline

A fundamental requirement of WeatherGPT is that the LLM is never the forecasting system. It interprets verified physical science.

[ User Query (Text or Voice) ] ↓ [ Intent Detection & Persona Profiling ] ↓ [ Verified Meteorological Data Fetching ] • Open-Meteo Feed & Surface Stations • Multi-Model Ensembles (ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS, IMD NWP) ↓ [ Weather Intelligence & Risk Engine ] • Computes Inter-Model Agreement & Operational Risk (Low/Moderate/High) • Calculates Convective Gusts & Cloudburst Probability ↓ [ Server-Side Gemini 3.8 Flash (via @google/genai) ] • Strictly constrained: "Interpret verified data. Never invent numbers." ↓ [ Dual Output Stream ] • Natural Language Actionable Advice • Verified Evidence Card + 1-Click Action Buttons

Resilience Feature: If API connectivity drops, an offline local rule-based intelligence engine automatically activates, ensuring uninterrupted service.

5 Key Product Modules & Live Demo Walkthrough
  1. 5-Second Hero Card: 29°C, Feels Like 31°C, Rain 62%, Humidity 71%, Wind 14 km/h, UV 6, with GPS auto-detection and city presets.
  2. Ask WeatherGPT: Immediate voice-enabled (🎙️) natural language query input with suggested questions directly below the hero card.
  3. Conversational Evidence Cards: Displays rain probability, expected millimeters, wind gusts, temperature, model consensus, and Text-to-Speech audio read-aloud.
  4. Diurnal Forecast Center: Responsive SVG temperature curves with hourly rainfall bars, plus 7-day outlooks with morning/afternoon/evening/night periods.
  5. Interactive Radar Map: Animated layers for Rain, Temperature, Wind streams, and Alert boundaries with a +30m/+60m/+90m timeline scrubber.
  6. Alert Center: Tiered Red/Orange/Yellow warnings with validity windows, meteorological root causes, and radius customization (10km / 25km / 50km).
  7. Agriculture Intelligence: Crop-specific advisories (Tomato, Rice, Wheat, Coffee) matching growth stages to soil moisture and pesticide application windows.
  8. Climate Explorer: 30-year surface temperature anomalies (1995–2026) contextualizing short-term weather within long-term climate change.
